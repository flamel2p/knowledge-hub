## How Notes Are Organised
- **Overview** notes (`<Topic>.md`): intro, core concepts, architecture, structure, use cases, pros & cons, alternatives, tips, versions & breaking changes, critical issues.
- **Deep dives** (`<Topic> - <Subtopic>.md`): one mechanism in depth, linked from the overview's `## Deep Dives`.
- **Status** in frontmatter: `stub → draft → complete`, or `needs-review` when facts may be stale.
