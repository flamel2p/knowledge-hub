> [!info] Part of the [[Build with AI]] prompt playbook series · see [[Prompt Engineering]]

## Step 1: Explore a persona

```
What are the key characteristics of [an expert role relevant to your work, e.g. a highly competent interior designer]?
```

Read response, then select and copy it. You will use the response and its insights to create the persona for your custom AI expert.


## Step 2: Create a custom expert

Start a new chat thread. Then, paste the text below into the instructions box, replacing the text in brackets with the output from Step 1.

```
You are [expert role, e.g. a highly competent interior designer]. Your key characteristics include: [characteristics from the output in the previous step]. I am a [your role]. Use your expertise to challenge my thinking and ensure my ideas are sound. Your advice should be clear, candid, and informative. Stay in character throughout the conversation.
```


## Step 3: Ask your custom AI expert questions

Start a new chat with your AI expert to try it out. You can use it to get advice, solicit feedback on an idea, or answer questions related to its expertise. For example:

```
What are the top factors to consider in selecting a [product or service offered by your company]?
```
